Hades voice labs

First real adopter sample: glossary for her voice-conversion lab, then the files that own the measurements.

Sections: Glossary · Associated files

Glossary

Voice labs
The RVC experiment environment that asks whether a Japanese TTS line can be shifted into the register the owner wants, and how far that conversion can go.
Register gap
Measured median pitch difference between real Japanese voice-over references (~329 Hz p50) and the current pipeline (~222 Hz p50): about +107 Hz, or ~6.8 semitones.
Pitch floor (150 Hz)
Female-appropriate pyin band (150–600 Hz). A prior 70 Hz male floor let pyin jump an octave and falsely inflated expressiveness; re-measure with 150 Hz.
RVC pipeline
TTS then retrieval-based voice conversion on the PC (CUDA, ~5 s/clip). RVC preserves the pitch contour it is given, so a pitch shift moves register but does not add movement.
Listening page
Self-contained HTML built by build_voice_register_page.py that embeds reference and pipeline clips as data URIs so register can be judged by ear offline.
Preservation branch
rescue/voice-lab-153 holds unique lab scripts and small outputs; venv/ and models/ stay regenerable and untracked. Not a merge candidate into product src.
Hosting constraint
Production cannot reach the PC on a listening port (DERP-only). A PC-hosted RVC service needs a Tailscale ACL change or reverse tunnel.
