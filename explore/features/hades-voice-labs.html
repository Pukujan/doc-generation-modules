Glossary

Voice labs
RVC experiment env: can a Japanese TTS line shift into the owner's target register, and how far?
Register gap
Median pitch gap: refs ~329 Hz p50 vs pipeline ~222 Hz p50 (~+107 Hz / ~6.8 semitones).
Pitch floor (150 Hz)
Female pyin band 150–600 Hz. A 70 Hz floor let pyin jump an octave; re-measure at 150 Hz.
RVC pipeline
TTS then retrieval VC on PC (CUDA, ~5 s/clip). RVC keeps pitch contour; shift moves register only.
Listening page
HTML from build_voice_register_page.py with data-URI clips for offline ear checks.
Preservation branch
rescue/voice-lab-153 holds unique scripts/outputs; venv/models regenerable. Not a product merge.
Hosting constraint
Prod cannot reach the PC listen port (DERP-only). Needs Tailscale ACL or reverse tunnel.
